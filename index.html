Geometry of Scoring in Soccer

How angle, distance, player position, and goalkeeper position affect a shot

In soccer, shooting is not only about power. The player’s position, the distance from goal, the visible shooting angle, and the goalkeeper’s position all change how open the goal looks. In this project I investigate these ideas using geometry, calculations, diagrams, and my own visual creation.
