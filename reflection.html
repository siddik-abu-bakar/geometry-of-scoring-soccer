Reflect — What changed in my thinking

This page connects the mathematics back to what I learned while doing the project.

What I first thought

At first I thought a player closer to the goal should always have the better chance to score. That was my starting idea before I made the diagrams and compared the positions.

What surprised me

The biggest surprise was that a player can be closer to the goal and still have a worse geometric chance if the player is too wide.

What I understand now

Now I understand that scoring opportunity depends on more than one thing. The visible angle matters, the distance matters, and the goalkeeper’s position matters too. I also learned that geometry can explain something from sports in a very concrete way.

Connection to the real world

This project changed the way I watch soccer. Now when I see a player getting close to the goal, I also think about whether the player is central or wide, and whether the goalkeeper is reducing the open angle.

Final conclusion

Player position affects both distance and shooting angle. Central positions usually create wider angles, while wide positions make the goal look narrower. Getting closer can help, but it does not always give the best geometry. Goalkeeper position can also reduce the available shooting space. A good scoring chance comes from several geometric relationships working together.
